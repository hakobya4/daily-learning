# Day 2, Task 10 -- Make a real open-source contribution

## THE TASK

Find one genuinely approachable open issue on GitHub and either open a
PR (even a small one -- docs, a typo fix, a small bug) or, at minimum,
a thoughtful comment/question on the issue showing you understood it
and scoped out an approach. This is unsolved by design: I'm not
picking your issue or writing your fix -- evaluating fit is part of
the skill.

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR YOUR JOB SEARCH

A real merged PR (or even an active, well-reasoned issue comment) on
someone else's codebase is a much stronger signal to employers than
another solo kata: it proves you can read unfamiliar code, follow a
project's conventions, and communicate with maintainers -- all things
a junior dev job actually requires day one.

## HOW TO EVALUATE A CANDIDATE ISSUE (checklist)

- [ ] Repo has recent commits (not abandoned)
- [ ] Issue isn't already claimed/assigned or half-solved in a stale open PR
- [ ] You can explain the ask in one sentence after reading it
- [ ] There's a CONTRIBUTING.md (or obvious PR pattern from recent merged PRs) you can follow
