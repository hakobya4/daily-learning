   combined experience.

   Merge overlapping (or touching) intervals, sorted by start.

   Caesar cipher encode/decode with wraparound, case preservation, and

   Find files with identical content (by hash) under a directory,
   grouped.

   Compare two directories' file listings: only-in-first,
   only-in-second, in-both.
7. **Git** -- `topics/git/day5-cherry-pick-practice.md`
   Cherry-pick a single commit from one branch onto another, leaving

    A fourth real-world issue/PR rep (Days 2, 3, and 4's Task 10 are
